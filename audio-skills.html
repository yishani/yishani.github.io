Current direction / Ongoing

Transferable Audio Skills

How can audio-language models develop reusable skills without updating their weights? I study learning through feedback and text optimization across speech, sound, and music.

audiotext optimizationself-improvement

The research questions

I am interested in how models use acoustic evidence for perception and reasoning, and how strategies developed through feedback can be reused across tasks and datasets. This includes studying both the potential and the limits of adaptation around a fixed model.

The broader aim is to understand how audio skills can accumulate and transfer, and how to evaluate whether an apparent improvement reflects more reliable audio understanding.

This is ongoing, unpublished work. Further details will be shared when the research is ready.

For related work on instruction-driven audio-language understanding, see StethoLM.
