Language, Bias & Human Preferences

Two earlier projects examine different sides of language model behavior: what models pick up from linguistic form, and how people respond to what models say.

language modelshuman preferences

The shared question

Language is never just a string of tokens. Negation can change what a sentence means; empathy and demographics can change which response a person prefers. These projects made me curious about how model behavior and human judgment meet, and about the care needed when evaluating both.
