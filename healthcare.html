AI for Health Signals & Care

I work on models and methods for health signals, from cardiopulmonary audio to ECG and other time series. I also collaborate with hospitals to connect research questions with clinical data and practice.

The research thread

Health signals are rich, noisy, and connected to decisions that matter. I am interested in methods that can learn from them, reason across modalities and tasks, and stay grounded in the evidence available. Working with hospitals helps shape the questions, data, and evaluation around real needs.
