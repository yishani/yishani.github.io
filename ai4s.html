Current direction / AI for Science

Agentic Harnesses for AI4S

How can a foundation model do more than produce a plausible scientific answer? I am interested in systems that keep the work itself alive: executable artifacts, empirical feedback, and a path for revision.

agentic AIharnessesAI4Ssimulationself-improvementtext optimization

The question

Scientific work rarely has one exact answer. A useful agent must explore alternatives, compare them with observations, preserve what works, and decide what to revise next. The harness around a model determines what it remembers, what feedback it can use, and how its next attempt differs from the last.

This is where my interests in agentic AI, recursive self-improvement, and text optimization meet AI for science. I want to understand which parts of a scientific workflow should live in language, which should live in code, and how evidence can guide their evolution.
